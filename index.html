Experience

Semiconductor and quantum-device fabrication

Semiconductor Nanofabrication

In industry, I have worked on the development and integration of multi-step fabrication processes for GaN-based microLED devices, spanning lithography, dry etching, dielectric passivation, metallization, thin-film deposition, process troubleshooting, and device integration.

Superconducting & Quantum Devices

During my PhD in condensed matter physics, I fabricated superconducting nanoscale devices and investigated quantum transport in electron-doped cuprate superconductors, integrating epitaxial thin-film growth, nanofabrication, structural characterization, and low-temperature transport.
