Hall-Bar Devices & Nonlinear Hall Transport

Fabrication and measurement of Hall-bar devices for longitudinal and transverse transport studies, followed by investigation of nonlinear Hall response and multi-carrier behavior in electron-doped cuprate thin films.

Design and fabrication of micron- and nanoscale superconducting rings using photolithography and electron-beam lithography, combined with microscopy and low-temperature transport studies.
