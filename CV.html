Research

Superconductivity and quantum transport

My doctoral research combined materials growth, nanofabrication, and cryogenic transport to investigate unconventional superconductivity in electron-doped cuprates.

I grew epitaxial superconducting oxide thin films using pulsed laser deposition, fabricated nanoscale devices using lithography and pattern-transfer techniques, and characterized their electronic response at low temperature and under magnetic field.

My research has included Little-Parks experiments, Hall-effect measurements, magnetoresistance, nonlinear transport analysis, and studies across different carrier concentrations.
